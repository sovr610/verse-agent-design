    Resident handbook

    What applicants ask the residency office most often.

    Who can apply?

    Anyone 16 or older who passes the clinic's intake exam. Families apply together on one form. Children under 16 move in with a parent or guardian and start at the vault school the following week.

    What does it cost?

    No caps. Every adult works a 36-hour weekly rota in hydroponics, maintenance, the clinic or security. Applicants with medical, electrical or water skills are placed first.

    Can I bring animals?

    One dog or cat per household, checked and vaccinated at intake. Brahmin and other livestock stay with the Hollow Creek herders on the surface. The vault buys their milk and meat at market rate.

    What happens if I'm turned down?

    You get the reason in writing and can reapply after 60 days. Most refusals are for untreated illness. The clinic will treat you first, then fast-track your next application.

    Is it safe from radiation?

    The top level sits 140 metres under the ridge. Surface air passes three filter stages, and the indoor dose rate is 0.02 rads per hour, lower than Hollow Creek's market square. Every resident wears a dosimeter badge.
